About Radiance Aesthetics

A studio devoted to
beautiful, healthy smiles.

Radiance Aesthetics is a dental and smile-enhancement brand dedicated to helping clients achieve healthier, more confident smiles. We combine careful clinical work with a calm, considered experience — so every visit feels less like a procedure and more like time spent on yourself.

From a routine clean to a full smile refresh, our focus stays the same: listen properly, explain clearly, and deliver work with real attention to detail.

  • Quality care, every detail Clean technique, careful workmanship and a finish we are happy to put our name to.
  • Comfort you can feel A relaxed setting, gentle handling and no pressure — you set the pace.
  • Customer satisfaction first We are not finished until you are genuinely happy with your smile.

Our Services

Everything your smile
could ask for.

Seven considered treatments, each tailored to you. Choose a service below and we will talk through exactly what it involves before anything begins.

01

Scaling & Polishing

A deep, gentle clean that lifts plaque and surface stains, leaving your teeth feeling smooth, fresh and noticeably brighter.

02

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening that lifts dullness and discolouration for a smile that looks naturally radiant — not artificial.

03

Fashion Braces

Modern braces with a style of their own — gently guiding teeth into better alignment while you make the look your own.

04

Curettage

Targeted gum care that clears away build-up below the gumline, helping your gums return to a healthier, more comfortable state.

05

Tooth Filling

Repairs for chipped or decayed teeth using tooth-coloured material, restoring strength and shape so it blends in seamlessly.

06

Tooth Gems

A small, beautifully set crystal that catches the light with every smile — a subtle piece of jewellery, applied safely and precisely.

07

Tooth Replacement

Restoring a missing tooth with a natural-looking replacement, bringing back comfortable chewing and a complete, even smile.
